Class 10 History – Chapter 1

The Rise of Nationalism in Europe

📅 Important Days & Dates

  • 1776: USA became an independent nation-state.
  • 1789: French Revolution began.
  • 1791: Switzerland became a nation-state.
  • 1804: Napoleonic Code was introduced.
  • 1815: Battle of Waterloo and Treaty of Vienna.
  • 1815: France came under conservative rule; Louis XVIII became king.
  • 1821: Greek War of Independence started.
  • 1824: Lord Byron died supporting the Greek struggle.
  • 1830: July Revolution in France; Belgium separated from the Netherlands.
  • 1831: Giuseppe Mazzini founded Young Italy and Young Europe.
  • 1832: Treaty of Constantinople – Greece became an independent nation-state.
  • 1833: Zollverein (customs union) formed in Germany.
  • 1834: Removal of economic barriers between German states.
  • 1848: France became a republic.
  • 1871: Germany unified; Kaiser William I became emperor.

🌍 Italy & Germany Before Unification

  • Italy: North ruled by Austrian Habsburgs, centre by the Pope, south by Bourbon kings.
  • Germany: Otto von Bismarck of Prussia led German unification.
